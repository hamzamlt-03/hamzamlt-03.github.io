- **Clinical Foundation:** Detailed breakdown of my clinical rotations and diagnostic laboratory expertise across high-volume healthcare settings.
- **Computational Projects:** Showcasing custom Python toolkits for DNA sequence analysis, PCR primer design, and SARS-CoV-2 sequence analysis utilizing Biopython.
- **Research & Publications:** Featuring my highly commended thesis on pediatric thalassemia and experimental in-vivo toxicology studies.
